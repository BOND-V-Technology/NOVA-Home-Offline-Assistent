# Privacy

Nova is built to keep your family's data at home.

## What stays on your device
- Your calendar, lists, notes, reminders, profiles and settings are stored
  only on your PC or Raspberry Pi.
- Voice runs locally: speech recognition (Whisper) and the spoken voice
  (Piper) work on the device. **No audio is sent anywhere.**
- The screen (HUD) loads nothing from the internet – no external fonts,
  scripts, maps or trackers.

## What leaves your device, and only if you choose it
- **Cloud AI (optional).** If you pick a cloud AI (for example Claude or
  another provider), your question is sent to that provider to be answered.
  Choose a local AI model instead and nothing leaves the house.
- **Weather (optional).** If weather is enabled, Nova asks a public weather
  service using only rounded coordinates.
- **Calendar links (optional).** If you add internet calendar links, Nova
  fetches them from their address.

Turn on **"Local only"** mode and Nova uses only local AI and your home
network – no cloud, no weather lookups, no internet calendars.

## No tracking
Nova contains no telemetry, no analytics, no advertising and no licence
"phone-home". It does not report usage to anyone.

## Your control
Everything is in files on your device. You can delete your data at any time,
and the backup feature keeps copies only where you tell it to.
